Sacred Heritage & Divine Chronicle

History & Sthala Purana

For over two centuries, this consecrated sanctum has served as a lighthouse of supreme peace, fulfilling the prayers of countless millions across generations.

Ancient Origin

The Swayambhu Manifestation

According to the ancient temple chronicles preserved by the lineage of head priests, the sacred Ganesha and Mahalakshmi idols were discovered untouched during an auspicious Kartik Purnima dawn excavation by revered saints.

The right-trunk (Dakshinabhimukhi) Ganesha murti is especially celebrated for granting Siddhis (spiritual and material attainments) to sincere devotees who circumambulate (pradakshina) the inner garbhagriha with pure devotion.

"Whoever bows before this altar with an unburdened heart receives the unceasing grace of the divine mother and the remover of all obstacles."
Sacred Geometry

Temple Vastu & Architecture

🛕

Constructed according to the ancient Agamic and Shilpa Shastra principles, the sanctum channels positive cosmic prana into the hearts of all visitors.

🌟

The Golden Shikhar

The 65-foot soaring gopuram is crowned with a solid brass and gold-leaf kalash that catches the first morning rays of the sun, charging the whole temple perimeter with energy.

🏛️

Carved Mandapa Pillars

32 intricately hand-sculpted granite pillars depicting the 32 divine postures of Lord Ganesha and the Ashta Lakshmis provide a grand procession walkway for devotees.

🔥

Akhand Yajna Shala

A dedicated Vedic sacrificial fire sanctum where daily Ganapati Homa and Sri Suktam Havans take place under continuous chanting of the Rigveda and Yajurveda.
