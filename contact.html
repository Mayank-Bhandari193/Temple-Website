Yatra Connectivity

How to Reach the Mandir

🧭
✈️

By Air

Mumbai Airport (BOM): Approx 14 km away. Direct prepaid cabs and app taxis available directly to Prabhadevi.

🚆

By Train

Dadar Junction: 1.8 km from temple. Connected to all major Indian cities. Local taxis reach temple in 8 minutes.

🚇

By Metro

Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line): Alight directly at Siddhivinayak Station (Gate 2 is within 250 meters walking distance).

🚗

By Road & Parking

Convenient multilevel automated parking facility located 100 meters adjacent to the temple with 400 car slots.
