Pilgrim Etiquette

Guidelines for Devotees

🪔
👔

Vedic Dress Code

Traditional modest attire is required. Dhoti / Kurta-Pyjama for men; Sarees or Salwar Kameez for women. Shorts, sleeveless, and Western beachwear are strictly not permitted inside the sanctum.

📱

Mobile & Electronics

Mobile phones must be switched to silent or switched off upon crossing the Garud Mandapa. Photography or videography inside the inner Garbhagriha is strictly prohibited.

🦽

Accessibility & Assistance

Complimentary wheelchairs, ramps, and dedicated volunteer assistants are available at Gate 2 for elderly pilgrims and differently-abled devotees.
